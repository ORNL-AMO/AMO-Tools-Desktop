Compressed Air Dryer Operating Cost Help
This calculator estimates the annual operating cost of a compressed air dryer based on the dryer type, operating conditions, schedule, and utility rates. Supported dryer types include heatless, heated externally, blower purge, heat-of-compression, and refrigerated designs.

Dryer Type
The dryer technology. The type sets which inputs apply and resets its type-specific inputs to default values.
  • Heatless: purge only.
  • Heated Externally: purge and heater.
  • Blower Purge (With Sweep): purge, heater and blower motor.
  • Blower Purge (Without Sweep): heater and blower motor, no purge.
  • Heat of Compression (HC): purge and a calculated heater.
  • Heat of Compression (SP): controls only.
  • Refrigerated: refrigeration motor and cooling water.
