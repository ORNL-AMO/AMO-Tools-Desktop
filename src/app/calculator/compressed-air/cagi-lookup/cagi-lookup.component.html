CAGI Lookup Help

Search the bundled CAGI compressor data sheets by model number on the left, and click a match to add it to the comparison table. Add as many as you like to compare units side by side, then use Copy Table to paste the selection into a spreadsheet.

Performance Comparison Graph

The graph plots each selected compressor's part-load performance. Variable-speed (VSD) units are drawn through every capacity/power point published on their data sheet; fixed-speed load/unload units have no part-load ladder, so they are approximated by a straight line between the full-load point and the zero-flow point.

Normalize each to 100% scales every compressor to its own full-load capacity and power, so the curve shapes can be compared directly. Scale to largest references every unit to the highest-capacity compressor in the selection: a unit with half the capacity ends at 50% on the airflow axis, and its power is shown as a percent of the largest unit's full-load power (so a less efficient small unit can read above 100% power — the axes expand to fit).

* The straight line for load/unload units is only accurate under ideal storage conditions. For a more accurate intermediate result, use the Compressed Air Assessment.
