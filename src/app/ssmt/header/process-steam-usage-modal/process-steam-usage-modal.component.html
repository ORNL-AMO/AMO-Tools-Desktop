Help

Process Steam Usage

Enter the number of steam leaks and failed steam traps on this header. Their estimated steam losses are calculated using the header pressure level. Add a row for each process or other steam use.

Condensate Recovery

Select Condensate Recoverable? only when condensate from that steam use can return to the system, then enter the percentage recovered for that row.

The overall recovery rate is the total recovered condensate divided by total process steam usage. Steam leaks and trap losses are excluded from recovered condensate unless explicitly marked recoverable.
